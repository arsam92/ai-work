# BLACK MILE — MISSION DESIGN

## ACT I — RETURN

### 01 — THE MESSAGE
Elias returns to Veyra after Jonah's final recording.
Purpose: establish the city, protagonist and central mystery.

### 02 — OLD KEYS
Someone searched June's garage before Elias arrived. June knows more than she says.
Possible approaches: calm conversation, pressure, independent investigation.

### 03 — NIGHT SHIFT
Elias helps a delivery driver cross Black Mile. A normal conversation reveals a name connected to Jonah.

### 04 — ELEVEN
An old report lists eleven victims. A bartender insists there were twelve.

## ACT II — THE CITY LIES

### 05 — THREE STORIES
Three witnesses describe the same night differently. The player decides who seems credible.

### 06 — DEBT
Viktor gives Elias a job. It can be completed, refused, negotiated or sabotaged.

### 07 — MARA'S SECRET
Elias discovers Mara has been talking to Detective Ortiz. The player can assume betrayal, but that assumption may be wrong.

### 08 — THE QUIET DRIVER
An old criminal recognizes Elias and says: You were not supposed to remember.

### 09 — THE WITNESS
A forgotten witness can only be reached through relationships the player has cultivated.

## ACT III — THE BROTHER

### 10 — JONAH
Elias reaches Jonah. Jonah asks: Do you remember what you drove that night?

### 11 — THE LEDGER
Jonah reveals a ledger connecting Kestrel, criminals, police and city officials.

### 12 — THE MISSING PAGES
Missing pages are distributed among different people. Possible solutions include trust, negotiation, theft, intimidation and betrayal.

### 13 — MARA
The truth about Mara's choices is revealed. The player's previous relationship with her affects the scene.

## ACT IV — THE LAST MILE

### 14 — NO CLEAN ALLY
Elias discovers every major faction has a hidden agenda.

### 15 — THE CITY HEARS
Elias has enough evidence to expose part of the conspiracy. Releasing it can also harm innocent people.

### 16 — THE LAST MILE
The final operation depends on previous relationships, information, faction status and world state.

## Mission Philosophy
Every important mission must change at least one of:

- NPC relationship
- information available
- world state
- faction behavior
- future mission availability
- NPC memory

Do not build missions as disposable errands.

## Choice Philosophy
Do not label choices good or bad.
Use meaningful trade-offs:

Choice A -> benefit + cost
Choice B -> benefit + cost
Choice C -> unknown consequence

Players should be allowed to make mistakes and live with them.
