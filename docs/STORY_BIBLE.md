# BLACK MILE — STORY BIBLE

## Core Identity

BLACK MILE is a 3D open-world crime drama set in the fictional city of Veyra.

The game is story-first. The player should care more about people, secrets, relationships and consequences than graphical fidelity.

Priority:
- Story: 50%
- NPC interaction and intelligent behavior: 29%
- Animation: 19%
- Graphics: 1%
- Stability: 1%

## Premise

Eight years ago, a violent event called Black Mile was officially recorded as a gang conflict. Eleven people were reported dead. The district was later cleared for redevelopment.

The official story is false.

A private corporation called Kestrel Dynamics helped engineer chaos around the district so land could be acquired cheaply. Criminal groups, corrupt officials and selected police officers became pieces of the operation.

Elias Ward, a former getaway driver, unknowingly helped move evidence on the night of the incident.

Years later Elias returns to Veyra after receiving a final recording from his missing younger brother Jonah:

> Do not come looking for me. Find the person who remembers Black Mile.

Jonah disappears.

Elias comes home to find him.

## The Central Mystery

The player begins with:

Where is Jonah?

Then:

Why was Jonah investigating Black Mile?

Then:

Who is lying?

Then:

What actually happened eight years ago?

Finally:

What should Elias do with the truth?

## The City

Veyra is a living 3D city rather than a collection of mission locations.

Important areas:
- Black Mile industrial corridor
- Harbor district
- Old Market
- Southside apartments
- Downtown business district
- Police precinct
- North hills
- Railway yards
- June's garage

NPCs should have routines and relationships so the city feels alive even when the player is not directly involved.

## Elias Ward

Age: 31

Former getaway driver and mechanic.

Traits:
- observant
- sarcastic
- practical
- emotionally guarded
- capable of violence
- uncomfortable with authority

Elias is not innocent and not the mastermind. He unknowingly played a role in the Black Mile cover-up. His greatest fear is discovering that the person he remembers himself to be is incomplete.

## Jonah Ward

Jonah is an investigator rather than a fighter.

He collected witness statements, recordings, payment trails, photographs and contradictory memories from the Black Mile incident.

He discovered that contradictions were not mistakes. They were evidence.

Jonah is alive, but he no longer completely trusts Elias.

## Factions

### Kestrel Dynamics

A powerful private corporation publicly focused on redevelopment, logistics and economic recovery.

Privately it is connected to the hidden Black Mile operation.

### Vanta Syndicate

A criminal organization with control over parts of the city's underground economy.

Viktor Hale leads it.

Vanta knows that powerful people used criminals as disposable tools, but not everyone inside Vanta agrees on what should happen next.

### Veyra Police

Some officers are honest. Some are compromised. Some know pieces of the truth but do not know the whole story.

### Civilians

Civilians hold fragments of the city's history through memories, rumors, family stories and personal experience.

## Narrative Rules

1. NPCs are not omniscient.
2. Every important NPC has limited knowledge.
3. Rumors may be false.
4. Important NPCs remember major player actions.
5. Relationships affect future dialogue and missions.
6. Major choices should have benefits and costs.
7. Players can miss information.
8. Missions should support multiple solutions.
9. The story should react to the player's behavior.
10. Twists must be prepared through earlier clues.

## NPC Knowledge Model

An NPC should only know information they legitimately observed, learned, inferred or were told.

Information can come from:
- direct observation
- conversation
- evidence
- another NPC
- faction communication
- media
- rumor
- surveillance

Conflicting information is allowed.

An NPC may believe something that is completely wrong.

## Rumor System

Player action -> witness -> conversation -> rumor -> faction response -> new behavior.

Rumors should mutate during transmission.

For example, Elias threatens one gang member. A witness tells a bartender. The bartender exaggerates the event. A gang contact hears that Elias attacked three people.

The player is now dealing with the consequences of something that is technically false.

## Dialogue Philosophy

Dialogue is stateful.

Relevant factors:
- trust
- fear
- suspicion
- respect
- affection
- previous promises
- previous lies
- player reputation
- current mood
- location
- time
- who else is nearby
- what the NPC knows

NPCs can refuse to answer.
NPCs can lie.
NPCs can manipulate.
NPCs can change their minds.
Silence can be a dialogue choice.

## Animation Philosophy

Animation should reveal information that dialogue does not explicitly reveal.

Examples:
- avoiding eye contact
- checking a door
- hesitation
- nervous hands
- stepping closer
- stepping away
- anger
- fear
- exhaustion
- fake confidence

A character can say that they are fine while their body language says otherwise.

## Main Story

### ACT I — RETURN

01. THE MESSAGE
Elias returns to Veyra after receiving Jonah's recording.

02. OLD KEYS
Elias visits June's garage and discovers someone searched the building.

03. NIGHT SHIFT
Elias helps a delivery driver cross Black Mile and hears a name connected to Jonah.

04. ELEVEN
Elias discovers an old report listing eleven victims while a bartender insists there were twelve.

### ACT II — THE CITY LIES

05. THREE STORIES
Three witnesses describe the same night differently.

06. DEBT
Viktor Hale offers Elias a job. The player may accept, refuse, negotiate or sabotage it.

07. MARA'S SECRET
Elias discovers that his sister Mara has been secretly talking to Detective Lena Ortiz.

08. THE QUIET DRIVER
An old criminal recognizes Elias and says he was not supposed to remember.

09. THE WITNESS
A forgotten witness becomes reachable through the player's relationships.

### ACT III — THE BROTHER

10. JONAH
Elias finds Jonah. Jonah asks: Do you remember what you drove that night?

11. THE LEDGER
Jonah reveals a financial ledger connecting Kestrel, criminals, police and city officials.

12. THE MISSING PAGES
The missing pages are spread across different people. Some can be gained through trust, others through deception or force.

13. MARA
The truth about Mara's secret choices is revealed.

### ACT IV — THE LAST MILE

14. NO CLEAN ALLY
Elias discovers that every major faction has a hidden agenda.

15. THE CITY HEARS
Elias can expose the truth, but doing so may harm innocent people.

16. THE LAST MILE
The final operation depends on relationships, information and previous choices.

## Endings

### THE TRUTH
The player exposes the conspiracy.

### THE KINGMAKER
Elias keeps the evidence and turns information into power.

### THE BURN
Elias destroys the evidence.

### THE BETRAYAL
The player sells the truth to a faction.

### THE LAST MEMORY
The rarest ending. Instead of one perfect document, hundreds of independent testimonies reconstruct the truth.

## Emotional Goal

The player should remember who trusted Elias, who he lied to, who he helped, who he betrayed, and which relationships changed because of him.

The intended reaction is not:

That cutscene was cool.

It is:

I caused that.

## Final Principle

BLACK MILE is not about having the biggest map.

It is about making the player believe that the people in the city have lives, memories and opinions that continue when the player walks away.
