# BLACK MILE — CHARACTERS

## Elias Ward
Protagonist. 31. Former getaway driver and mechanic.
Traits: observant, sarcastic, practical, guarded, capable of violence, uncomfortable with authority.
Core conflict: Elias wants the truth about Jonah, but fears discovering that his own past is worse than he remembers.

## Jonah Ward
Missing younger brother and investigator.
Traits: analytical, patient, obsessive, cautious, morally serious.
Jonah collected testimony and evidence about Black Mile. He is alive, but he no longer completely trusts Elias.

## Mara Ward
Elias's younger sister and paramedic.
Traits: compassionate, stubborn, protective, secretive.
She has been protecting Elias while hiding information from both criminals and police. Her choices can put Jonah in danger.

## Detective Lena Ortiz
Detective who worked on the original Black Mile case.
Traits: disciplined, skeptical, intelligent, controlled.
She knows the official case was manipulated but does not know the whole truth. She may become an ally, reluctant ally, neutral contact or adversary.

## Viktor Hale
Leader of the Vanta Syndicate.
Traits: patient, polite, strategic, dangerous.
He treats favors as long-term investments and remembers both loyalty and disrespect.

## June Mercer
Mechanic and owner of a struggling garage.
Traits: practical, funny, loyal, financially stressed.
June knew Elias before his conviction. Helping her can create a strong alliance, but the player may need to make difficult choices to save her garage.

## Samuel Cross
Kestrel Dynamics executive and respected public businessman.
Traits: charismatic, calm, intelligent, socially respected.
He believes Veyra must be controlled to remain stable. His methods are ruthless, but his philosophy should remain understandable.

## Malik 'Ghost' Rahmani
Street informant and occasional dealer.
Traits: charming, paranoid, opportunistic, cowardly under direct pressure.
Goals: remain useful to Vanta and get his sister out of the city. His information is valuable but not perfectly reliable.

## Relationship Design
Important NPCs must track multiple relationship dimensions rather than a single favorability score:

- Trust
- Fear
- Respect
- Anger
- Loyalty
- Affection
- Suspicion

A relationship can become contradictory. An NPC can fear Elias while respecting him. An NPC can love him while suspecting him.

## Character Rule
Every major NPC must have goals, fears, secrets, memories, relationships, knowledge, misinformation, emotional state, routine, and something they want from the player.

Characters must continue having motives and routines when the player is not watching.
